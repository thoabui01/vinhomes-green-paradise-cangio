From the precision of Berlin’s Michelin-starred kitchens to the fluid elegance of Parisian front-of-house operations, I have dedicated my life to mastering the "German Standard" of operational discipline.

My approach is not merely about management; it is about the philosophy of service. Every concept I develop is rooted in heritage yet optimized for the technical demands of the 21st century.
